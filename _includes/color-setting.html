The choice is saved in the browser, shared by both appearances, and put on as data-color="dark" or "light"; geo/color-init.html and minimal/color-init.html apply it on each page load. Params: default the colors before a visitor picks: "dark" (GeoCities) or "system" (Minimal: the device's setting) {%- endcomment -%}
