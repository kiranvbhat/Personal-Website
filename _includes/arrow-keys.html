{%- comment -%} Keyboard shortcuts for section pages (page-nav.html in geo/ and modern/ includes this): left, right go to the previous and next sections, by following the links at the bottom of the page up, down only with `entries=true` (Modern): move between the page's entries (projects, jobs, ...) and, on Education, the coursework subjects after them. Each is scrolled into view and focused, which modern.css shows (fading the other entries, like hovering, or outlining a subject). A subject opens when moved to and closes when moved away from (Enter also opens or closes it). Past the first or last, up and down scroll the page as usual. Held down, they move on as fast as the page can scroll. Without it (GeoCities), up and down just scroll the page. Ignored when shortcuts are turned off in settings (shortcuts-setting.html), while typing (e.g. in the guestbook), in anything that uses the arrow keys itself (like a dropdown or a video), and with Shift, Ctrl, Alt or Cmd held, so the browser's own shortcuts still work. {%- endcomment -%}
