{%- comment -%} Keyboard shortcuts for section pages (page-nav.html in geo/ and minimal/ includes this): left, right go to the previous and next sections, by following the links at the bottom of the page up, down only with `entries=true` (Minimal): move between the page's entries (projects, jobs, ...). Each one is scrolled into view and focused, which minimal.css shows by fading the others, like hovering. Past the first or last entry, up and down scroll the page as usual. Held down, they move on as fast as the page can scroll. Without it (GeoCities), up and down just scroll the page. Ignored when shortcuts are turned off in settings (shortcuts-setting.html), while typing (e.g. in the guestbook), in anything that uses the arrow keys itself (like a dropdown or a video), and with Shift, Ctrl, Alt or Cmd held, so the browser's own shortcuts still work. {%- endcomment -%}
