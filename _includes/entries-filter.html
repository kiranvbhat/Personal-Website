{%- comment -%} The Featured / All switch at the top of section pages (a .toggle, like the settings). Featured, the default, shows only the entries marked `featured: true` in _data/; All shows every entry. The choice is saved in the browser and shared by both appearances (entries-init.html applies it on each page load). The CSS shows the switch only on pages that have both featured and other entries, and hides a sub-heading (like Other's "Photography") whose entries are all hidden. Included under the page title by geo/page-title.html and the Minimal layout. {%- endcomment -%}
