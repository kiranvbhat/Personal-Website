{%- comment -%} Bandcamp players (.bandcamp-player, from geo/media.html and modern/entries.html) in light or dark colors to match the site. The colors are part of the player's address, so changing them reloads it, which would stop a song that's playing. The page can't see inside the player, so once a visitor clicks into one it keeps its colors until the next page load. Each appearance's layout includes this with its own colors. Params: dark, light the player's colors in each mode, as they appear in its address: "bgcol=000000/linkcol=ffff00" (the background, which Bandcamp also uses to pick the text color, and the links' color) system true: with no color picked in settings, follow the device's light or dark mode (Modern). Otherwise dark unless light is picked (GeoCities). {%- endcomment -%}
