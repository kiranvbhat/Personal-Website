{%- comment -%} Special effects for cursors with an `effect` in _data/cursors.yml (cursor-init.html puts it on as data-cursor-effect): paint hold the mouse button and drag to draw in rainbow colors. The drawing stays put on the page until you leave it. It's drawn on a canvas placed in the page (so it scrolls with it), only as tall as the drawing reaches. saber a short-lived glowing red trail behind the cursor (skipped for visitors who turn off animations), drawn on a canvas fixed over the window. None over links and buttons, where the cursor turns into the helmet. explosion each click sets off an explosion (assets/graphics/misc/explosion 5 frames.png, the frames of explosion 5.gif in a strip, so it always plays once from the start; see .cursor-explosion in geocities.css). Explosions go in a page-sized layer, so they scroll with the page. Skipped for visitors who turn off animations. Clicks go straight through both canvases. Devices with a mouse only. {%- endcomment -%}
