{%- comment -%} Clicking a YouTube thumbnail (.video-poster, from media.html) replaces it with the video player, already playing. The player comes from youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed, so nothing from YouTube but the thumbnail image loads until a visitor chooses to play a video. Thumbnails start as the sharp 1280x720 one, but not every video has it (YouTube sends back a 120x90 placeholder), so those switch to the 480x360 one every video has. {%- endcomment -%}
