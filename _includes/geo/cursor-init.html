{%- comment -%} Applies the cursor picked in the cursor menu (cursor-setting.html), before the page draws. The choice is saved in the browser as the cursor's id from _data/cursors.yml; visitors who haven't picked one get defaultSiteCursor below, and "system" means the browser's own cursor. Also defines setSiteCursor(id), which the menu calls. Sets --site-cursor, --site-link and (for cursors that have one) --site-text on ; geocities.css uses them on devices with a mouse. For cursors with an `effect`, also sets data-cursor-effect on (see assets/js/cursor-effects/core.js). {%- endcomment -%}
